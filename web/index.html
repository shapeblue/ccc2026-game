CloudStack Card Clash

Build the cloud before the clock runs out.

Each round gives you a scenario, a budget and 150 seconds to design an Apache CloudStack architecture that solves it. Then an incident hits, and we see if your design survives.

  1. 1Read the scenario and its list of requirements.
  2. 2Tap cards to add them to your design. Some cards only work if others are in place, like VPN needing a VPC.
  3. 3Deploy. Points for every requirement met, budget saved and seconds left, plus a bonus if you survive the incident.
